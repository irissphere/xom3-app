# Individual/Family (Private Plans) Follow-Up Sequence

**Lead Type:** Individual/Family (Private Plans - PRIMARY FOCUS)  
**Total Messages:** 5 (including intake confirmation)  
**Total Duration:** ~8 days

---

## Sequence Overview

1. **Intake Confirmation** (Immediate)
   - Template: `intake-confirmation-individual`
   - Timing: Immediate upon intake
   - Purpose: Acknowledge inquiry, set expectations, include plan name if detected

2. **First Follow-Up** (2 hours after intake)
   - Template: `followup-1-individual`
   - Timing: 2 hours after intake
   - Purpose: Initial outreach, schedule call, reference specific plan if known

3. **Second Follow-Up** (24 hours after intake)
   - Template: `followup-2-individual`
   - Timing: 24 hours after intake
   - Purpose: Re-engage, check-in, offer value, explore alternatives

4. **Third Follow-Up** (3 days after intake)
   - Template: `followup-3-individual`
   - Timing: 3 days after intake
   - Purpose: Final push, urgency around enrollment period

5. **Check-In** (7 days after intake)
   - Template: `check-in-individual`
   - Timing: 7 days after intake
   - Purpose: Long-term check-in, maintain relationship

6. **Enrollment Reminder** (2 days before deadline)
   - Template: `enrollment-reminder-individual`
   - Timing: 2 days before enrollment deadline
   - Purpose: Urgency reminder, final call-to-action

## Message Themes by Sequence

### Intake Confirmation
- Acknowledge inquiry
- **Include specific plan name if detected** (e.g., "United Premiere Advantage")
- Set response expectation (2 hours)
- Emphasize private plan options

### First Follow-Up
- Personal introduction (agent name)
- **Reference specific plan if known** (shows we remember their interest)
- Emphasize exploring options (flexibility of private plans)
- Clear call-to-action (YES or call)

### Second Follow-Up
- Re-engagement
- Offer additional value/information
- Explore alternatives to requested plan
- Alternative call-to-action

### Third Follow-Up
- Create urgency (if enrollment period)
- Explore all options
- Final push for engagement

### Check-In
- Long-term relationship maintenance
- Offer ongoing support
- Low-pressure check-in

### Enrollment Reminder
- Urgency around deadline
- Final call-to-action
- Reference specific plan if applicable

## Special Considerations for Private Plans

### Plan Name Detection
- **Always include detected plan name** in messaging when available
- Shows personalization and attention to detail
- Builds trust that we understand their specific need

### Private Plan Keywords
- Emphasize: flexibility, choice, personalized service
- Avoid: marketplace, subsidy (these are ACA-specific)
- Focus on: direct options, comprehensive coverage, individual needs

### Common Private Plans
- United Premiere Advantage / United Premier Advantage
- Premiere Advantage / Premier Advantage
- Secure Advantage
- Premiere Choice / Premier Choice

## Opt-Out Handling

- Every message includes opt-out instruction
- If client opts out at any point:
  - Stop sequence immediately
  - Log opt-out with timestamp
  - Update client record
  - Do not send further SMS

- Include agent name in follow-ups
