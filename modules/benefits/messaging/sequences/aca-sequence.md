# ACA Follow-Up Sequence

**Lead Type:** ACA  
**Total Messages:** 5 (including intake confirmation)  
**Total Duration:** ~8 days

---

## Sequence Overview

1. **Intake Confirmation** (Immediate)
   - Template: `intake-confirmation-aca`
   - Timing: Immediate upon intake
   - Purpose: Acknowledge inquiry, set expectations

2. **First Follow-Up** (2 hours after intake)
   - Template: `followup-1-aca`
   - Timing: 2 hours after intake
   - Purpose: Initial outreach, schedule call, emphasize subsidies

3. **Second Follow-Up** (24 hours after intake)
   - Template: `followup-2-aca`
   - Timing: 24 hours after intake
   - Purpose: Re-engage, check-in, offer value

4. **Third Follow-Up** (3 days after intake)
   - Template: `followup-3-aca`
   - Timing: 3 days after intake
   - Purpose: Final push, urgency around enrollment

5. **Check-In** (7 days after intake)
   - Template: `check-in-aca`
   - Timing: 7 days after intake
   - Purpose: Long-term check-in, maintain relationship

6. **Enrollment Reminder** (1 day before deadline)
   - Template: `enrollment-reminder-aca`
   - Timing: 1 day before enrollment deadline
   - Purpose: Urgency reminder, final call-to-action

## Message Themes by Sequence

### Intake Confirmation
- Acknowledge inquiry
- Set response expectation (2 hours)
- Emphasize subsidy eligibility

### First Follow-Up
- Personal introduction (agent name)
- Emphasize subsidies (key value prop)
- Clear call-to-action (YES or call)

### Second Follow-Up
- Re-engagement
- Offer additional value/information
- Alternative call-to-action

### Third Follow-Up
- Create urgency
- Enrollment period awareness
- Final push for engagement

### Check-In
- Long-term relationship maintenance
- Offer ongoing support
- Low-pressure check-in

### Enrollment Reminder
- Urgency around deadline
- Final call-to-action
- Consequences of missing deadline (if applicable)

---

## Opt-Out Handling

- Every message includes opt-out instruction
- If client opts out at any point:
  - Stop sequence immediately
  - Log opt-out with timestamp
  - Update client record
  - Do not send further SMS

---

## Personalization Guidelines

- Always use first name
- Reference specific ACA/marketplace context
- Emphasize subsidy eligibility consistently
- Include agent name in follow-ups
- Reference previous interactions when applicable
